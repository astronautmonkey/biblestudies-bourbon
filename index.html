Weekend afternoons · Saturday afternoon and all day Sunday

None of this is set in stone. Just some ideas if you're looking for something to do. Go with whoever, do as much or as little as you want, and feel free to suggest your own. Drive times from the house are rough.

Minutes from the house
~10

Four Roses

Lawrenceburg

Right outside town. Sundays roughly noon–4.

~10

Wild Turkey

Lawrenceburg

Up on the hill over the Kentucky River. Check their site for hours.

~20

Castle & Key

Near FrankfortBooked · Sun 3:15 PM

An old stone castle distillery with a garden trail you can walk without a tour. Sundays about 11–5.

~25

Woodford Reserve

Versailles

Old stone buildings out in horse country. Tastings by reservation; Sunday about noon–4:30.

~25

Buffalo Trace

Frankfort

Big campus with free tours. Sundays 11–5, and it gets busy, so earlier is better.

With 15 of us, tours may need to be split into smaller groups or booked ahead. Pick a designated driver per car.

Other
ideas

  • Downtown Frankfort ~20 min

    Walk the State Capitol and the floral clock, grab bourbon balls at Rebecca Ruth Candy, and poke around the shops along the river.

  • Lexington ~40 min

    Downtown restaurants and coffee, and you can drive past the horse farms on the way. Keeneland's grounds are worth a stop even with no racing.

  • Kentucky Horse Park North of Lexington · ~45 min

    Horses, barns and museums. Winter hours are Fri–Sun, 10–4, so both weekend days work.

  • Louisville ~1 hr

    A bigger-city day: the riverfront and the walkable Big Four Bridge, the Louisville Slugger Museum, Whiskey Row downtown, or the Kentucky Derby Museum at Churchill Downs.
