On the road

Gas

Down and back is split within each car. For territory days, save your receipts: we'll total them at the end of the week and split them evenly.

On the road

Go easy on fragrance

Some of us are sensitive to strong scents, and we'll share cars for hours. Please skip strong perfume, cologne and air fresheners on the drive.

Weekend

Bourbon, maybe

Saturday afternoon and all day Sunday. Nothing set in stone: go with whoever, do as much or as little as you like. Pick a designated driver per car.

Lexington

Downtown restaurants and coffee, with the horse farms on the way.

~1 hr · Riverfront

Louisville

Big Four Bridge, the Slugger Museum, Whiskey Row or the Derby Museum.

In the territory

What to offer

  • Offer the Enjoy Life Forever! brochure. If the householder already has it, any other literature may be offered.
  • Carry a variety of tracts for not-at-homes and for anyone who doesn't accept literature.
  • Each car has a box of literature and bags for not-at-homes. Keep anything left discreet, tucked out of sight.
  • Try to start a Bible study on the first call. Make return visits on strong interest.

Interest

Recording interest

  • Name
  • Address or location
  • City, state and ZIP
  • Telephone number
  • Placement and remarks · Travis fills out the S-277 afterward

Just in case

If police stop us

  • Don't discuss legal rights. Leave politely and promptly if asked.
  • Then Travis and an elder from a neighboring congregation contact the Legal Department.
Territory 2578

Our patch of Kentucky

Mostly Spencer County and the north end of Nelson, with small corners of Bullitt and Jefferson. About 30–45 minutes from the house.
